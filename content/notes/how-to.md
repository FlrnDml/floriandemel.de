# How to write a blog

## Before starting

1. **Sketch a structure** that has bullet points what you want to write. A clear guide to follow while writing.

2. **Start with one sentence**, not a topic. "Event-driven architecture" is a topic. "Most teams adopt Kafka before they have a problem Kafka solves" is an article. If you can't state your core claim in one sentence, you're not ready to write. Everything in the piece should serve that sentence.

3. **Define the reader precisely**. Pick one person: what they already know, what they're struggling with, and what they should be able to do or think differently after reading. This decides your depth, jargon level, and which examples you use. Writing for "everyone" produces generic text.

## Structure 
The most common structure for a blog article looks like this:

Title: a specific promise or claim, ideally under ~70 characters.
Hook (1–3 sentences): a problem, surprising fact, question, or short anecdote that makes the reader care.
Intro / context (1 paragraph): why the topic matters, what the article covers, and what the reader will get out of it.
Body (3–5 sections with subheadings): each section makes one point, supported by an example, data, code, or a visual. Sections should build on each other logically.
Counterpoints or limitations (optional but valuable): where your argument doesn't hold, trade-offs, common objections.
Conclusion: a short recap of the key insight, not a repetition of every section.
Call to action: what the reader should do next, such as try something, read further, comment, or share their experience.
